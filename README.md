# School Pulse

## Overview

School Pulse is an anonymous student wellbeing check-in platform for schools.

Students can share their stress level and main concerns without providing their names. Schools see aggregated insights instead of individual responses, helping them understand common problems and support students early.

## Problem

Students may experience stress from exams, academic workload, relationships, bullying, or future plans. Schools may not always know which problems are affecting students most.

School Pulse creates a simple way to collect anonymous student feedback and turn it into useful school-wide insights.

## How It Works

### For Students
- Complete a short anonymous check-in.
- Select their stress level and main stressors.
- Receive supportive resources based on their responses.

### For Schools
- View aggregated student wellbeing data.
- Identify common stressors and trends.
- View early warning signals.
- Explore possible preventive actions.

## Privacy

Privacy is a core part of School Pulse.

- No student names are displayed.
- Schools see aggregated data.
- Small groups are hidden to help protect privacy.
- The platform is designed for support, not diagnosis.
